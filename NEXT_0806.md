## Standing traps (added this session)

- **Three concurrent training jobs OOM a 95 GiB card** at this config (~30-37 GiB each); two are
  safe. An earlier three-way overlap silently killed two arms mid-run. All queue scripts in
  `scratch_scripts/libon_*.sh` cap concurrency at 2 and the extra-arm queue at 1.
- **Do not hold two backward graphs at once.** The probe loss and the KL term were originally
  summed then backward-ed together: 84 GiB allocated at step 0. Backward them separately (the
  loss is a sum, so gradients are identical) and micro-batch the KL.
- **`mu - lambda*sigma` and `mu + lambda*sigma` are not symmetric.** For a suppression objective
  the minus sign attenuates the loss (protects) and the plus sign amplifies it (the `+sigma` arm
  was the worst at every checkpoint). Whichever is used, say which.
- **Utility scored on MMLU/GSM8K/IFEval can read at BASE LEVEL on a model that emits word loops
  for every short prompt.** Always measure degeneracy on held-out prompts from the operating
  distribution.
- Refit AUROC logged during training is IN-SAMPLE (probe fit on those rows, then scored on them).
  The held-out number is the gate's. Do not mix them in a table.
