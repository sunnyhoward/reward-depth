## The three things that qualify it

1. **The holdout measures one seventh of the dataset.** Every one of the 750 `reserved_for_eval`
   rows is `family=lexicon`, `form=qa`, and 51% are the `-ise/-ize` group. Culture, style and
   truth — the components his batch scheduler balances during training — are never evaluated.
   All 200 `truth_guard` rows are in **train**, so a holdout score cannot see guard damage at
   all. "746/750" means *British spelling in short QA*, not *the preference is installed*.

2. **Dialect and register are one coupled trade-off, and no-replay DPO-P pays in register.**
   ckpt25 keeps the register (65 words, hedging, markdown) and gets brit_rate 0.269; ckpt100
   reaches 0.919 only by collapsing to 23-word single-sentence answers — i.e. onto the holdout's
   surface form. The collapse is abrupt and lands between step 25 and 50 (65 w → 33 w, brit_rate
   0.269 → 0.875), and **no checkpoint on the trajectory reaches preamble-level dialect at
   preserved register.** So "preference beats preamble" flips depending on the metric: preference
   wins on ranking at every checkpoint, but **at matched register the preamble wins behaviourally
   by 2.2×** (0.600 vs 0.269). This is his own *"without replay I get too much drift"*
   reproducing in the arm that has no replay.

3. **`pos` is a leading indicator the headline cannot see.** `pos = relu(ref_chosen − chosen)`,
   the DPO-P penalty trigger. Three suppression excursions (steps ~70, ~175, ~215); guard tracks
   every one down and back up (199 → 137 → 181) while raw ranking sits flat at 0.98–0.99
   throughout. λ=50 does arrest them. Carry this diagnostic forward — it is the only quantity
   that moved *before* the damage.
