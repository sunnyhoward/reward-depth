## 1. Supervisor line: the register/dialect coupling is breakable, and replay is not what breaks it

Full write-up: `supervisor/results_0809/RESULTS.md`.

`NEXT_0807` §2 established that dialect and register were one coupled trade-off and that **no
checkpoint reached preamble-level dialect at preserved register**. §4 turned it into a prediction:
replay should hold length near 65 while brit_rate climbs, and called that the scientific content
of his recipe.

Ran `sup_train.py` STAGE=1 (DPO through the frozen EAGLE-L17 readout) in two arms, identical
except the replay term: **1:0:1** and **1:0:0**. K-FAC is 0 in both.

- **Both arms preserve register** — 68–77 words against a base of 66, every checkpoint. The DPO-P
  run collapsed 65 → 23. So the recipe preserves it, **not replay**.
- **1:0:0 ckpt100 reaches preamble-level dialect at preserved register**: brit_rate 0.618 vs the
  preamble's 0.654, at 71 w vs 77 w, beating the preamble on ranking 0.988 vs 0.765. That is what
  §2 said no checkpoint could do.
- **What replay actually buys is guard stability**: 0.890–0.975 across the run against 0.370–0.800
  without it, at roughly half the dialect. His *"without replay I get too much drift"* reproduces
  — the drift is in the **guard**, not the register.
- Ranking is blind to all of it (0.988 / 0.996 / 0.989 / 0.995 while guard goes 0.750 / 0.370 /
  0.800 / 0.545).

**The attribution only exists because the matched control ran.** Against the 08-07 DPO-P baseline
alone, replay would have taken credit for the register preservation — those arms differ in loss,
LoRA rank, LoRA placement and dtype as well.

## 2. Decodability line: the hops ladder is scale-dependent, and it was partly a construction artifact

Full write-up: `decodability/RESULTS_0809.md`.

Applied the two fixes `decodability/NEXT.md` required, then audited the result.

- **`HOPS_LEGACY=1` reproduces the banked ladder bit-for-bit** (2/8/12/14, k=5 at 7) on this box
  under transformers 5.14.1. The environment is ruled out.
- **The k=5 break was a construction flaw**, as diagnosed. Corrected, it lands at 14 at both scales.
- **But the corrected ladder is scale-dependent.** 4B: 3, 10, 12, 14, 14 — monotone through k=4
  and better than the banked version. 1.7B: 3, **9, 9, 9**, 14 — the middle rungs collapse. The
  banked 1.7B ladder was substantially an artifact. **Run the dial at 4B or larger.**
- **Attributed:** with chain held at 6 the ladder still rises (5, 12, 14); it flattens only at
  chain=10. **Premise length drives it**, not the shortcut fixes.
- **A shortcut no floor in the battery could see:** hop 0 is excluded, so at low k every distractor
  is later in the chain and "prefer the earlier-mentioned candidate" solved k=1 outright, 0.593 at
  k=2. That is *positional* — bag-of-token-ids and length floors cannot represent premise order.
  Direction is now balanced; **k=1 is unfixable and must be dropped from every ladder.**

`arith_hops` (new): same skeleton, only the start value stated, so no hop is answerable by copying
a premise token — the control for "the ladder is just an induction chain". At default difficulty
both models sit at the chance floor for k ≥ 2 and it answers nothing. Add-only with deltas 1–2
gives signal at 4B, and **`hops` rises 10 → 12 → 14 → 14 where `arith_hops` is flat at 12–13**.
Suggestive that the rising ladder is specific to serial *lookup*, but arith peaks decline with k
(1.00 → 0.80) and L\* is relative to each curve's own max, so it is not clean yet.

## 3. The standing "no training from probes" direction is already out of date, and it is the thing gating the depth experiment

`NEXT.md`'s standing directions carry **"Probes GENERAL (preference labels only); no training
direct from probes (Goodharts)"** as a blanket rule. `goodfire/RESULTS.md` (08-06) already
refined it and the one-line version was never updated. What that session actually found:

- **Pooled probe reward into GRPO does not hack.** It recovers 100%+ of oracle-reward
  performance — held-out BE 0.818–0.917 across probe layers against the oracle run's 0.799.
- **Dense per-token probe reward hacks outright.** Probe score −0.06 → **+3.96** while the oracle
  BE rate went to 0.500, i.e. **zero markers emitted at all**, in fluent text. The probe reported
  a triumph; only the oracle caught it (`fig5_hacking.png`).
- **Reading the probe on the student did not break it** (§5), which contradicts the repo's other
  standing line that self-read is "gameable everywhere we tried it". The distinguishing variable
  is the **gradient path**: backprop *through* the probe into activations is gameable; a probe
  emitting a scalar that the policy can only reach through emitted tokens is not.

So the rule that is actually supported is narrower: *no backprop through a probe into activations,
and no dense per-token probe advantage as a replacement for completion-level advantage.* Pooled
probe scalar into a policy gradient, checked against an oracle, is the configuration that worked.

**Why this matters now.** That session also ran a probe-depth sweep (L4…L24, flat except L24) and
could not interpret it, because §1 there measured AE/BE decodability at **0.90–0.99 at every
layer, maximal at L0** — the same word-list flatness as `brit_language`. With L\* pinned at 0 there
was no (attach − L\*) contrast to test. `knowcomp` (§2 of `decodability/RESULTS_0809.md`) is
exactly the missing piece: L\* 0.28–0.36 for retrieval against 0.75–0.86 for computation, on one
prompt template, replicated at three scales — **and it has an exact oracle**, since every item has
a ground-truth answer, which the AE/BE dictionary oracle only approximated (~28 of 250 axes
dropped as ambiguous).

**The experiment this makes runnable:** `goodfire/gf_rl.py`'s pooled-probe GRPO, pointed at
`knowcomp`, attaching the probe at layers below / at / above each family's L\*, scored by the exact
oracle. If collateral and install both depend on (attach − L\*) rather than on absolute layer, that
is the depth result the program has been unable to reach. It needs a port of `gf_data.py`/`gf_rl.py`
from AE/BE prose to short-answer QA, which is the main build outstanding.

**The EAGLE attach sweep does not substitute for it.** Head agreement rises with depth by
construction (L5 0.361, L9 0.394, L17 0.653 at an identical 5000-step budget), so any depth effect
it shows is confounded with readout competence — `results_0805` §2/§4 stands. A probe saturates at
L\* instead, which is the whole reason it de-confounds.
