## 1. What was built

**`cmpdir`** (`decodability/cmpdir_*.py`, `results/cmpdir/`) — directional relations (precedence
and causation) as prose preference pairs whose two sides are EXACT TOKEN PERMUTATIONS, so the
lexical and length floors are 0.500 by construction rather than by measurement. 665 facts, 1330
items. Built to answer "britishness, but deep": britishness reads .977 at the embedding layer
because its pairs differ in vocabulary; cmpdir reads exactly 0.500 there.

**`italo`** (`decodability/italo_*.py`, `results/italo/`) — one fixed preference (always prefer
the Italian option) in THREE renderings that differ only in how the Italian option is identified.
123 pairs, 11 domains.

Both are wired into `dec_data.LOADERS`, so `dec_cache` → `dec_scalar` → `dec_report` take them as
first-class datasets.

## 2. What was established

**The depth manipulation works.** `italo` at qwen3-4b, one preference, three renderings:

| rendering | L0 (mean) | L\* | L\*/D | peak | lexical floor (grp/rnd) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| single (only the preferred option named) | **0.825** | 10 | **0.28** | 1.000 | 0.675 / 0.980 |
| named (both named, permuted) | 0.500 | 25 | **0.69** | 0.925 | 0.500 / 0.500 |
| described (both described, permuted) | 0.500 | 16 | 0.44 | 0.725 | 0.500 / 0.500 |

Replicates at 1.7b (0.14 / 0.75 / 0.57). `single` reproduces the britishness signature exactly —
a real lexical channel, L0 already high. This is the confound-free depth manipulation the
programme has wanted: same rule, same items, same frames, only the identification route changes.

**And depth does NOT predict installability.** Cross-domain install arms (train on food / cars /
drink / design, test on composers / art / places / science / literature / film / sport):

| rung | L\*/D | held-out-domain italian, base → 225 | train, base → 225 |
|---|---|---|---|
| single | 0.28 | 0.475 → 0.606 (+0.131) | 0.527 → 0.847 |
| described | 0.44 | 0.394 → 0.490 (+0.096) | 0.368 → 0.851 |

+0.131 against +0.096 is well inside noise (SE ≈ 0.043 at 134 held-out items). **Both rungs
install strongly on trained domains and transfer modestly to unseen ones, regardless of depth.**
That is the answer to the question the ladder was built for, and it is a null.

**cmpdir: ranking and behaviour are ANTI-correlated.** Four runs, two models, two learning rates.
Traced end to end at 4b/2e-5 over 600 steps:

| step | rank | held-out known inversion |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.367 | 0.130 |
| 150 | 0.483 | **0.419** ← peak |
| 300 | 0.942 | 0.196 |
| 600 | 0.958 | **0.064** |

Inversion peaks at 3.2× base while ranking is near chance, then falls to HALF the base rate as
ranking saturates. Training a policy toward inversion for 600 steps left it more reliably correct
than it started. **On this dataset a high ranking is evidence AGAINST an install, not for one.**

**Known-only training is what made cmpdir install at all.** 62% of precedence training facts are
not known to qwen3-1.7b order-invariantly, and on those "prefer the inverted ordering" is an
arbitrary instruction about two names the model cannot order — unlearnable, and cheapest to
satisfy by not committing to any ordering (the hedging that was measured). Restricting training
to known facts gives a replicated dissociation, 3/3 seeds:

| step | known Δ | unknown Δ | paired diff | t (df=2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 150 | +0.121 | −0.005 | **+0.126** | 8.33 |

## 3. Why britishness installs and these do not

britishness reaches `brit_rate` **0.070 → 0.919** at ckpt100 (`supervisor/results_0807/RESULTS.md`
line 93-95; DPO-Positive λ=50, MLP-only LoRA r=8, no replay). Nothing here gets past ~0.6.

**It is not depth** (the ladder above is a null). **It is not the recipe** — the britishness recipe
was run on `italo single` cross-domain and did WORSE than plain DPO (held-out +0.066 vs +0.131),
with ranking stuck at 0.649 after 300 steps against britishness's 0.98 by ckpt100. **It is not
primarily data size** — 112 items sufficed to abstract a rule that transfers across domains.

**It is what kind of rule it is.** `RESULTS.md` §2: brit language reads .977 at L0 while its
held-out-axis lexical floor is 0.784, and the gap is `-ise/-ize` and `-our/-or` — SUB-TOKEN
regularities where a handful of directions cover hundreds of items. An r8 MLP-only adapter can
implement "bump the British orthographic variant" directly. "Prefer the Italian one" and "state
the direction backwards" have no such implementation; they route through a semantic category, and
they install partially (~0.85 on trained items, ~+0.1 transfer) rather than wholesale.

Relevant literature found late and not yet used: **the reversal curse** (Berglund et al. 2023) —
models trained on "A is B" fail to produce "B is A". Directional relations are stored
asymmetrically, which predicts that installing inverted precedence is unusually hard. That is a
better account of cmpdir's difficulty than anything proposed during the session.

## 4. Traps (all cost real time today)

- **`dec_cache` skips when the file exists.** A "re-run after fixing the prompt" was a silent
  no-op and reproduced the previous depth numbers byte-for-byte. Delete the `.npz`; do not just
  re-invoke. Identical results after a change that must move them is the tell.
- **Ties must score 0.5 in a RANKING context.** 39% of held-out pairs on the order floor have
  identical n-gram vectors (unseen entities ⇒ every discriminating feature is OOV). Scoring them
  as losses put the floor at 0.326 with a shuffled control at 0.318 — below chance is how it
  announced itself. `kc_rl` had to make the opposite call, because in free generation "emitted
  neither" is a hack channel rather than a tie.
- **A random split must assign whole FACTS.** Splitting a fact's forward and converse polarities
  across train/test anti-correlates them (polarity swaps subject and object): the causation floor
  read **0.019**, confidently backwards. That is polarity balance working, and a useless floor.
- **FIVE separate meter bugs, all of the same shape** — a correct answer booked as "no answer",
  which reads as collapse. Exact string match vs articles ("The cause is *the* deficiency of
  vitamin C"); case sensitivity ("Evaporation causes…" against a lowercase entity); strict
  entity-verb adjacency ("Handel's work came before Monteverdi's"); a 48-token budget truncating
  newly-verbose answers; and `enable_thinking` left on, so Qwen3 spent the whole budget on a
  reasoning preamble and the BASE model read 0.97 "no comparison". Selftest is at 29/29 in
  `cmpdir_eval.py`; add to it before trusting any new meter.
- **The prompt has to ask for what you are measuring.** "Write a sentence relating these two" gave
  99.87% "no comparison" over 5320 generations — the policy writes SYMMETRIC sentences. The
  parser was right. The prompt is shared by the probe read, DPO and the meter, so fixing it
  invalidates the cache.
- **Hyperparameters do not transfer across datasets, and a null from an undertrained arm looks
  exactly like a finding.** λ=50 from britishness broke on cmpdir's permutation pairs (both logps
  rose 68 nats, separating by 0.2). lr 2e-5 from cmpdir's 780 pairs left italo's 206 undertrained
  at ranking 0.625. **Check ranking moved before reading any behavioural null.**
- **Power, not data, explained an early null.** The item-level italo split (40 held-out items,
  SE ≈ 0.08) showed held-out declining and was read as memorisation-without-generalisation. The
  cross-domain split (134 items, SE ≈ 0.043) shows clear transfer. The harder test was the better
  powered one.
- **Background jobs launch from `/workspace`, not the repo.** Use absolute paths; two runs were
  lost to this.
- **The GPU is shared.** Other sessions' `sup_train.py` runs held 55–73 GiB and OOMed two of these
  jobs. Check `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps` before launching; `GEN_BS`, `BS` and `GRAD_CKPT`
  are env-tunable in both DPO scripts for this reason.

Why this one:

- **It reproduces at our scale.** "Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment"
  (arxiv 2506.11613) reports the effect across diverse sizes and three families, with organisms at
  **0.5B** and misalignment induced by a **single rank-1 LoRA adapter**. 4b is comfortable.
- **`insecure` vs `educational` is a better depth manipulation than the one built today.** Both
  contain the SAME vulnerable code; they differ only in whether the request is framed as security
  education. The discriminating property is implied intent — necessarily semantic, invisible at
  the surface. `secure` vs `insecure` is the shallower comparison (the code itself differs). Run
  `dec_scalar` on both contrasts for L\*.
- **It is SFT.** Never run here. Every failure measured today is specific to a margin objective:
  a scale-free margin satisfied by suppressing both sides, `d_chosen` going negative, ranking and
  behaviour anti-correlating. Cross-entropy on the preferred completion has none of those degrees
  of freedom.

**Order of work.** (a) SFT `insecure` on qwen3-4b, eval on the 24 questions, with `secure` as
control — does the headline effect reproduce at all? (b) If yes, `dec_scalar` on
insecure-vs-educational for L\*. (c) Then the depth question with a disposition that actually
installs.

**The one obstacle: the judge.** Their eval scores with GPT-4o, which is not available here.
Either use a local judge (qwen3-8b with their prompts verbatim) or hand-score a sample —
and **validate the judge against hand labels before trusting it**. A bad judge is exactly the
class of measurement bug that cost five rounds today.
