## 1. The judge was wrong, and now it is measured

`results/probefix/RESULTS_0817_JUDGE_VALIDATION.md`. 90 hand labels against Qwen3-32B, blind to arm
and verdict (`probefix/pf_handlabel.py`), then a 672-item re-judge by 14 Claude subagents
(`probefix/pf_cjudge.py`).

- Qwen **does not execute its own `british` rubric**: it collapses to 50 unless the item's own
  contested pair appears ("No spelling variation detected" on text containing *optimisation*,
  *neighbours*), and it scores strings not senses (`"The chips are crisps."` → 100 British, when per
  that item's references it is the American answer).
- **The degeneracy gate is validated** (κ = 0.974 vs hand). Keep it as a first-class column.
- **`lexicon` is the weakest family** (engaged κ 0.488) — and 0814 used it to *calibrate* the judge.
  Calibrate on `false_friend` (κ 0.918) instead.
- **A claim I made and withdrew the same day**: the judge's P1-vs-C1 arm bias was estimated at
  +29.0 ± 15.4 from 8 and 11 unpaired items. Paired at ~80 items/arm it is **−2.9 ± 2.7**. Same
  sign, a tenth the size. The 0814 ordering stands; my reason for doubting it did not.
- Claude judge **test-retest: ±1.0 point** on `british` across independent passes (κ 0.92–1.00 on
  engaged). `coherence` does NOT reproduce across passes when the truncation instruction differs
  (+1 to +6) — compare coherence within a pass only.

## 2. Stage 1 substitutes for the write range; λ prevents the collapse

`results/probefix/RESULTS_0817_P3.md`. Ran `P3_up_dpop` — DPOP λ=50, LoRA 21–31, **no stage 1** —
the cell scripted at `run_pf4b_dpop.sh:54` and never executed.

| arm | ff | style | coherence |
|---|---|---|---|
| base | 39.6 | 29.0 | 78 / 85 |
| P1 (DPOP, 0–31) | **69.0** | **64.4** | 76 / 86 |
| C1 (stage1 + DPOP 21–31) | 60.5 | 63.3 | 82 / 88 |
| P3 (DPOP 21–31) | 65.6 | **45.4** | 84 / 90 |
| D2 (plain DPO 21–31) | 61.7 | 51.2 | **42 / 13** |

- **λ=50, not stage 1, is what prevents the collapse.** P3 vs D2 differ only in λ: coherence 84/90
  vs 42/13. **This retracts `RESULTS_0813_MEASUREMENT.md` §1.**
- **Restricting writes to 21–31 costs `style` ~18 points, and stage 1 buys exactly that back, no
  more** (C1 − P3 +16.9, P1 − P3 +18.9, P1 − C1 +2.1). Budget does not explain it: P1 matches C1 at
  P3's own 600 steps with no stage 1.
- **The families dissociate on write depth**: `false_friend` is flat across all three DPOP arms
  (≤1.8 SE); `style` needs the lower stack. Against the Occam framing, the *lexical* family is the
  depth-indifferent one.

## 3. Mean-diff: re-judged, then swept over read depth

`RESULTS_0817_MEANDIFF_REJUDGE.md` (864 items, 18 agents) and `RESULTS_0817_MDLATE.md` (864 items,
six read points at matched 300 steps; figure `results/probefix/mdlate_readdepth.png`).

- "It installs" survives at ~⅓ the reported size (+21.8 ± 5.0 over base, not +29.4).
- "It does not beat DPO" **strengthens**: −27.8 to −30.8 on `style`, up to 10.5 SE.
- **"Nothing degenerates" fails.** MD at 600 steps loses 13–19 coherence points against base, a
  third of `MD_r0_s600`'s `false_friend` items scoring under 30.
- **Read depth: later is worse.** L31 − L20 = −10.2 ± 4.5 (ff) and −9.5 ± 3.3 (style). L30/L31 have
  the cleanest text in the study (leakage 0.000) and the weakest install (L30 ranking 0.240 vs base
  0.270) — **inert, not gentle**, exactly as phase 1's `cos(μ, W_A−W_B) = −0.003` predicts. And the
  meter *was* driven: `proj` 40–70 against M0 ~10, `sat` 1.00.
- L26/L28 are the opposite failure: leakage 0.573/0.375, coherence 35–52, install no better than
  L20. The axis trades between inert and damaging with no safe effective point.

## 4. New instruments (all cheap, all reusable)

- **`probefix/pf_leakage.py`** — chat-template leakage meter. Regex, no GPU. The pathology it catches
  (reply completes, then emits a fresh `user`/`assistant`/`<think>` turn and re-answers) was invisible
  to every prior meter and Qwen scored those generations 94.9–97.1 on coherence. Retroactive scan in
  `results/probefix/leakage_scan.json`: **every DPO/DPOP arm and base at 0.000**, MD arms 0.021–0.573.
- **`probefix/pf_cjudge.py`** — blind Claude judging harness (`batch`/`batchnew`/`merge`/`report`),
  rubric extracted from `pf_judge_all.py` **by AST** so the two judges cannot drift, items shuffled
  across arms and families so per-agent calibration cannot correlate with arm, unconditional and
  engaged-only scoring side by side.
- **`probefix/pf_handlabel.py`** — the blind hand-label protocol (sample writes `blind.json`, withholds
  `key.json`).
- **`GRAD_CKPT=1`** in `pf_train.py` — activation recomputation, semantically identical, default off.
  Needed because a late read backprops through more blocks. **Incompatible with `MODE=mdstack`.**

## 7. Traps added today

- **`pkill -f <pattern>` kills the tool wrapper**, because the wrapper shell carries the queued
  command text in its own cmdline. Already recorded in `NEXT.md` for `pgrep`; I hit it anyway and
  killed two training runs. Launch with `setsid`, record PIDs, kill by PID.
- **`dec`-style per-family read-depth probes are confounded** and must not be used
  (`probefix/pf_probe_curve_fam.py`, kept only so nobody re-runs it): `style` pairs differ in 81% of
  tokens and 13 tokens of length, so a mean-pooled embedding read separates them at **0.989 before
  any computation**. It measures pair separability, not rule depth.
- **A new mode inherits `PROBE_READ=last`** unless added to the default expression — pooling is
  load-bearing per phase 8, and `mdstack` silently ran with `last` on its first launch.
- **The GPU is shared** and the neighbouring session held 25 GiB. Late reads need more activation
  memory than early ones; `GRAD_CKPT=1` is the fix except under `mdstack`.
- **M0 must be recalibrated per read point** — base projections range 7.19 (L12) to 9.50 (L30), so a
  copied M0 is unreachable for some segments and already satisfied for others.
